# Contributing

Thanks for helping make this list better.

## What gets in

- You have used the tool yourself, for real marketing work.
- It solves a problem marketers actually have.
- You can explain why it matters in one line.

## How to suggest something

1. Fork this repo and edit `README.md`.
2. Add your entry at the bottom of the right section, in this format:
   `- [Name](https://link) - One-line verdict, ending with a full stop.`
3. Open a pull request saying how you have used it.

Or just [open an issue](../../issues/new) with the link and your one-line verdict.

## What doesn't get in

- Affiliate or referral links.
- Tools you built yourself, unless you say so in the pull request.
- Anything that encourages fake reviews, fake followers or misleading AI content.
