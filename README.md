# Awesome AI Marketing [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A working marketer's list of AI tools, automation platforms and reading, with a one-line verdict on each.

Curated by [Rahul Sharma](https://github.com/RjRahulSharma01), founder of [Instant Production](https://instantproduction.in) and co-founder & CMO of [Hireonix AI](https://hireonixai.com). Thirteen years in content, social and digital growth. Everything here is something I use, have tested, or would hand to my own team. Short lists beat long ones, so if a tool isn't here, I either haven't tried it or didn't think it earned a place.

## AI assistants

- [Claude](https://claude.ai) - My daily driver for strategy, long-form writing and building tools. Connectors and skills turn it from a chatbot into a teammate.
- [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) - Broad and fast. Worth knowing well because your audience uses it.
- [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com) - Strongest when your work already lives in Google Workspace.
- [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai) - Research with sources attached. Good first stop before writing anything factual.
- [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com) - Drop in your research and ask questions of it. Great for turning reports into briefs.

## Automation and workflows

- [n8n](https://n8n.io) - The one I build on. Visual workflows, self-hostable, and strong AI nodes. Fair-code licence, so read it before reselling.
- [Zapier](https://zapier.com) - Easiest place to start. Gets expensive at volume.
- [Make](https://www.make.com) - Visual and affordable for multi-step scenarios.
- [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) - The open standard that lets AI assistants use your tools and data. Learn the idea even if you never write a server.

## Content and copy

- [Claude Projects](https://claude.ai) - Keep your brand guide and past work in one project so every draft starts on-voice.
- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com) - Still the best last pass on tone and clarity for a whole team.
- [Descript](https://www.descript.com) - Edit audio and video by editing the transcript. Changed how I think about podcasts.
- [Notion AI](https://www.notion.com/product/ai) - Useful when your content calendar and briefs already live in Notion.

## Images and design

- [Canva](https://www.canva.com) - Where most marketing design actually happens. Brand kits keep teams consistent.
- [Adobe Firefly](https://firefly.adobe.com) - Generative images trained with licensing in mind. The safer choice for client work.
- [Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com) - Best for striking concept and mood imagery.
- [Figma](https://www.figma.com) - For landing pages and anything a developer will build from.

## Video and audio

- [HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com) - AI avatars and translated video. Label it clearly as AI where your market requires it.
- [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io) - Voiceovers and dubbing that sound human. Get consent before cloning anyone's voice.
- [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com) - Fast short-form editing with auto-captions.
- [Opus Clip](https://www.opus.pro) - Cuts long videos into shorts. A good first draft, never the final cut.
- [Runway](https://runwayml.com) - Generative video for effects and b-roll.

## SEO and AI search

- [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) - Free, first-party data. Start here before paying for anything.
- [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com) - Backlinks, keywords and competitor research. Now tracks brand mentions in AI answers too.
- [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com) - The all-rounder for SEO and paid search research.
- [Schema.org](https://schema.org) - Structured data helps both search engines and AI assistants understand your pages.

## Analytics and measurement

- [Google Analytics 4](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/) - The default. Set up conversions properly on day one or regret it later.
- [Looker Studio](https://lookerstudio.google.com) - Free dashboards on top of Google data.
- [PostHog](https://posthog.com) - Product analytics, session replay and A/B tests in one, open source.
- [Umami](https://umami.is) - Simple, privacy-friendly web analytics you can self-host.

## Email and audience

- [Beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com) - Newsletter platform built for growth, with a referral programme built in.
- [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com) - Familiar and fine for small lists.
- [Listmonk](https://listmonk.app) - Open-source, self-hosted newsletters with no per-subscriber fees.

## Building your own tools

You don't need an engineering degree to build marketing tools any more. You need a clear problem and an AI pair-programmer.

- [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) - Describe what you want and build it with an AI that reads and writes the code with you. How most of my repos were made.
- [Vercel](https://vercel.com) - Put a website or small app live in minutes, with preview links for every change.
- [GitHub](https://github.com) - Where your work lives, gets versioned, and becomes your portfolio.
- [Streamlit](https://streamlit.io) - Turn a Python script into a shareable data app.

## Open-source to self-host

- [Ghost](https://ghost.org) - Publishing and newsletters in one. MIT licence.
- [Astro](https://astro.build) - Fast content sites and blogs. MIT licence.
- [Evidence](https://evidence.dev) - Build reports and dashboards from SQL and Markdown. MIT licence.
- [GrowthBook](https://www.growthbook.io) - Feature flags and A/B testing. MIT core.

## Reading and learning

- [Anthropic prompt engineering guide](https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/overview) - The clearest guide to writing prompts that work.
- [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs) - What Google actually says about SEO, from the source.
- [n8n workflow templates](https://n8n.io/workflows/) - Thousands of ready workflows to learn from and adapt.
- [Think with Google](https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com) - Consumer research and marketing data, useful for decks.
